Conditions We Treat

Brightlight Oasis is compassionate and dedicated to treating a diverse range of mental health conditions - from depression and anxiety to bipolar disorder, ADHD and PTSD.

Major Depressive Disorder

Persistent sadness, hopelessness and loss of interest that can interfere with daily functioning.

Anxiety Disorders

Excessive worry, fear or nervousness that feels hard to control and interrupts everyday life.

ADHD

Patterns of inattention, impulsivity or hyperactivity that affect school, work and relationships.

Bipolar Disorder

Mood shifts that include emotional highs, depression and changes in sleep, energy or focus.
